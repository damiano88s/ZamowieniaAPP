Privacy Policy – ZgłoszeniaAPP

Last updated: 13.05.2026

1. General Information

“ZgłoszeniaAPP” is an internal application designed for handling technical reports, maintenance tasks, snow removal reports, utility meter readings, bulky waste reports, and other property management related operations.

The application is intended only for authorized internal users.

2. Data That May Be Processed

The application may process data entered by the user, including:

  • user name,
  • report addresses,
  • report descriptions,
  • utility meter numbers,
  • utility meter readings,
  • technical information related to reports,
  • photos added by the user.

3. Camera and Photos

The application may use the device camera to take photos related to reports, technical documentation, snow removal, utility meter readings, or other activities performed within the application.

Photos are used only for the proper operation of the application and report handling purposes.

The application does not use the camera in the background and does not take photos without the user’s knowledge.

4. Data Storage

Data sent from the application may be stored in systems used by the application administrator, including services such as Google Sheets and Google Drive, solely for report handling and documentation archiving purposes.

5. Data Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Access to the data may only be granted to authorized personnel responsible for system administration and report handling.

6. Security

The application uses security mechanisms intended to protect data against unauthorized access.

7. Contact

For questions regarding the application, please contact the system administrator.
